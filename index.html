Understanding The Threat

Not Feeling Better Quickly Despite Taking Medicines?

The reason could be Antimicrobial Resistance (AMR). Understand the key facts through these 3 essential campaign questions:

1

What is AMR?

When antibiotics lose their effectiveness against germs and bacteria, that condition is called Antimicrobial Resistance (AMR).

🧫 Germs ➔ 💊 Antibiotics ➔ 🦠 Resistant Germs

Over time, resistant germs multiply and standard medicines stop working completely.

2

Why is AMR Dangerous?

  • 💊
    Medicines Become Ineffective: Standard drugs fail to cure common infections.
  • ⏳
    Prolonged Illness: Infections take much longer to recover.
  • 🏥
    Complex Treatments: Medical care & procedures become high-risk.
  • 💰
    Rising Costs: Treatment & hospital expenses increase.
  • 🚨
    Severe Risk: Risk of severe complications & mortality rises.
3

What Can We Do to Prevent AMR?

  • 🩺
    Doctor's Advice Only: Take antibiotics strictly when prescribed by a certified doctor.
  • 📅
    Complete Full Course: Always finish the complete treatment course prescribed.
  • 🗑️
    Safe Medicine Drop-Off: Deposit leftover or expired medicines into designated collection boxes or nearby health centers. Safe disposal protects our environment and stops AMR!

Campaign Event Roadmap

Interactive chronological timeline documenting key consultative meetings, research studies, state stewardship workshops, and innovation challenges across Maharashtra.

JAN 2026 – MAY 2026

✓

National Consultative Meeting

Key stakeholders deliberated on systemic issues and policy challenges around safe antibiotic disposal and suggested potential solutions. Report published.

Mar 2026
✓

KAP Study Completed

Households, pharmacists, and care-providers surveyed to map the systemic gaps and behavioural determinants of safe disposal.

Mar 2026
✓

State-level consultation Meeting

High-level state consultation bringing together healthcare leaders, DHS Maharashtra officials, and public health partners to establish actionable policy frameworks. The National Consultative Report was formally presented and disseminated to key health stakeholders.

Mar 2026
✓

Statewide Antimicrobial Stewardship (AMS) Workshops

KAP findings disseminated; health-system actors across the state sensitised on AMS practices and engaged for next steps.

May 2026
✓

Innovation Challenge Launched

Open call for digital & social innovations amplified through partner networks. Multidisciplinary jury panel completed evaluations, selecting 2 digital solutions and 1 social innovation for state implementation.
