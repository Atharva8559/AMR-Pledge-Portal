Expert Perspectives

Quotes & Testimonials

Expert Voice

“The fight against antimicrobial resistance begins not only in hospitals, but in every home. Every unused or expired antibiotic disposed of responsibly is one less risk to our health and environment. Let us make safe disposal a habit today—so that effective antibiotics remain available for tomorrow.”

Professor Y. K. Gupta

President, AIIMS - Kalyani. Principal Advisor India Strategy Development (GARDP). Former President of AIIMS Bhopal and AIIMS Jammu. Ex-Dean, AIIMS Delhi.

Expert Voice

“Antimicrobial resistance is one of the most pressing public health challenges of our time, and addressing it requires action beyond healthcare settings. Effective AMR containment requires a whole-of-society approach that combines awareness, stewardship, and responsible disposal practices. The Maharashtra initiative is an important example of how partnerships between government, communities, and development organizations can translate AMR commitments into tangible action. Together, we can strengthen public understanding and protect the effectiveness of life-saving antimicrobials.”

Dr Bassem Zayed

Unit Head & Regional Advisor, Antimicrobial Resistance (AMR), Health System Department (HSD), WHO (SEARO).

Expert Voice

“Safe disposal must become part of prescribing behaviour, just as much as the right drug, dose and duration. Every antibiotic prescription should come with a clear message—not only about how to take the medicine, but also how to safely dispose of what remains. Responsible antibiotic use does not end with the last dose; it ends with safe disposal.”

Kamini Walia

Senior Scientist at ICMR.

Expert Voice

“Safe disposal of antibiotics is critical to protect us from consumption of unnecessary regular dosages of antibiotics through the food chain. Saving environmental contamination is key to our fight against AMR and we must all participate”

Ankur Mutreja

Director Health Security and External Affairs, PATH

Expert Voice

“Addressing antimicrobial resistance requires us to look beyond the use of antibiotics to how unused and expired antibiotics are disposed of. Through meaningful community engagement and accessible disposal options, we aim to make responsible disposal an everyday practice. Lasting behavior change depends on communities being informed, involved, and empowered to make safer choices that protect public health and the environment.”

Dr. Asha Hegde

Country Director- India, Director- South Asia Hub, PATH

Project Milestones

Campaign Event Roadmap

Jan 2026

National Consultative Meeting

Key stakeholders deliberated on systemic issues and policy challenges around safe antibiotic disposal and suggested potential solutions. Report published.

Mar 2026

KAP Study Completed

Households, pharmacists, and care-providers surveyed to map the systemic gaps and behavioural determinants of safe disposal.

Mar 2026

State-level consultation Meeting

High-level state consultation bringing together healthcare leaders, DHS Maharashtra officials, and public health partners to establish actionable policy frameworks. The National Consultative Report was formally presented and disseminated to key health stakeholders.

Mar 2026

Statewide Antimicrobial Stewardship (AMS) Workshops

KAP findings disseminated; health-system actors across the state sensitised on AMS practices and engaged for next steps.

May 2026

Innovation Challenge Launched

Open call for digital & social innovations amplified through partner networks. Multidisciplinary jury panel completed evaluations, selecting 2 digital solutions and 1 social innovation for state implementation.

Jul 2026

District Workshop and Bin Placement Initiative Launched — Pune

A district-level workshop brought together medical officers, municipal officials, and pharmacy association representatives to strengthen awareness on safe antibiotic disposal and AMR prevention. Following the workshop, dedicated antibiotic disposal bins were installed at strategic locations across Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC) to enable safe collection and disposal of unused and expired antibiotics.

Aug 2026

District Workshop and Bin Placement Initiative Launched — Satara

Key district stakeholders convened to discuss implementation strategies and promote safe antibiotic disposal practices across Satara. To support community action, antibiotic disposal bins were subsequently placed at selected healthcare facilities and public access points, improving access to safe medicine disposal and reducing environmental contamination risks.
